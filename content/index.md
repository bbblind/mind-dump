# Welcome to My Mind

*"One must still have chaos in oneself to be able to give birth to a dancing star."*

## Who Am I?

A wanderer between thoughts, a collector of fragments, a builder of meaning from the scattered pieces of experience.

This is not a blog. This is not a portfolio. This is a **digital garden** — a living, breathing space where ideas grow wild and unrestrained.

## What Is This?

Think of this as my **external brain** — a place where I cultivate thoughts, observations, and insights. Some notes are polished essays. Others are raw fragments, seeds waiting to bloom.

Here you'll find:
- 🎬 **Cinema** — reflections on films that moved me
- 🎵 **Music & Lyrics** — the sounds that shape my soul  
- ✍️ **Poetry** — attempts to capture the ineffable
- 💭 **Thoughts** — everything else, unfiltered

## Why This Exists

> *"No artist tolerates reality." — Nietzsche*

I create because I must. I write to think, and I think to understand. This garden is my laboratory, my studio, my sanctuary.

The notes here are **interconnected** — follow the threads, see where they lead. There is no linear path, only exploration.

## A Warning

These notes are **unfinished by design**. They evolve. They contradict. They question.

If you seek polished truths, you've come to the wrong place. But if you hunger for **authentic thinking-in-progress**, welcome.

*Explore. Question. Create.*

**— Andrea**
