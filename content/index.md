# Hi, welcome to my digital garden 🌱

This is my personal knowledge base where I share my thoughts, notes, and learnings.

## What is this?

This is a **digital garden** - a collection of interconnected notes that grow and evolve over time. Unlike a blog with polished articles, this is a living document of my learning journey.

## Explore

Use the explorer on the left to browse through different topics, or use the search bar to find specific content.

Some notes may be:
- 🌱 Seedlings: Just planted, rough ideas
- 🌿 Budding: Growing, being developed
- 🌳 Evergreen: Mature, well-developed thoughts

## Connect

Feel free to explore and see how ideas connect. The graph view on the right shows the relationships between different notes.
