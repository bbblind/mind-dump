# Welcome to Your Digital Garden 🌱

This is your personal knowledge base.

## How to Use

**In Obsidian:**
1. All your notes go in the `content/` folder
2. Create folders to organize (cinema, music, poetry, etc.)
3. Auto-syncs every 5 minutes to GitHub
4. Your site updates automatically!

## Your Structure

I can see you've started with:
- 🎬 cinema/
- 🎵 music/ & lyrics/
- ✍️ poetry/
- 💭 thoughts/

Just create `.md` files in these folders and they'll appear on your site!

## Quick Start

1. Right-click on a folder → New note
2. Name it (use dashes for spaces: `My-First-Note`)
3. Write your content
4. Save (Cmd+S)
5. Wait 5 min or Cmd+P → "Obsidian Git: Backup"

Your URLs will be: `https://bbblind.github.io/mind-dump/Your-Note-Name`
